Student Visa FAQ — 2026
How much is the student visa 500 fee in Australia 2026?
The student visa subclass 500 application fee is AUD $2,000 per primary applicant from 1 July 2025, up from $1,600. A partner aged 18+ also pays AUD $2,000. Each dependent child under 18 pays AUD $500. The fee is non-refundable if the application is refused.
How much does the 485 graduate visa cost in 2026?
The 485 Temporary Graduate Visa fee is AUD $4,600 from 1 March 2026, doubled from $2,300. Partners aged 18+ pay $2,300. Children under 18 pay $1,150. Budget for this from the first day of your studies — do not wait until graduation.
Can I apply for a partner visa while on a student visa in Australia?
Yes. You can apply for a partner visa subclass 820 while holding a student visa in Australia. You must be in Australia at time of application. The subclass 820 application fee is AUD $9,095. Processing typically takes 12–24 months onshore.
How many hours can a student work in Australia 2026?
Student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session. During official scheduled course breaks there is no cap. Masters by Research and PhD students have no work hour restriction at any time.
What is the Genuine Student Requirement for Australia?
The Genuine Student (GS) requirement replaced the GTE statement on 23 March 2024. DHA assesses 6 factors: study commitment, compliance with visa conditions, financial circumstances, immigration history, personal circumstances, and home country ties. Check your GSR risk free →
What is the PR pathway from a student visa in Australia?
Typical pathway: 500 student visa → 485 graduate visa (2–4 years) → skills assessment → EOI via SkillSelect → 190 state nomination or 491 regional nomination → permanent residency. Only 39% of international students achieve PR — planning from year one makes the difference. Check your EOI queue →
