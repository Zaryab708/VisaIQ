Common questions
EOI queue FAQ
Does submitting my EOI early improve my chances? +
No — and it can hurt you. DHA selects by points score, not submission date. If you lodge below the likely cutoff and get invited later, your Date of Effect resets to the invitation date, not your original submission. Only lodge when your score is at or above the cutoff.
How long is the realistic wait in 2026? +
It varies enormously by occupation and score. Healthcare Tier 1 at 75 pts averages 3–6 months. Engineering averages 6–12 months. Accountants and ICT can wait 12–24+ months — many received zero federal 189 invitations in 2025–26.
What's the fastest legal way to move up the queue? +
Three fastest moves: NAATI CCL adds 5 points in ~90 days for ~$800. Superior English adds 10 points over Proficient (IELTS 8.0+ all bands). The 491 regional pathway adds 15 points and has separate, often faster invitation pools than federal 189.
Can I hold both 189 and 190 EOIs simultaneously? +
You can lodge separate EOIs for 189 and 190, but can only hold one state nomination at a time. The smart move is to lodge your 189 EOI immediately if above the cutoff, and simultaneously register interest with states actively nominating your occupation — SA and TAS are most active in 2026.
