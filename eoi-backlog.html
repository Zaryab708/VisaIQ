EOI backlog — common questions

What is the SkillSelect EOI backlog?
The SkillSelect EOI backlog is the number of Expressions of Interest currently in the pool waiting for a skilled visa invitation, broken down by occupation and points score. A larger backlog at or above your points score means a longer expected wait.
How many EOIs are in the Australian skilled migration pool?
As at June 2026, VisaIQ tracks over 143,550 EOIs across more than 60 occupations in the subclass 189 pool. Large occupations like Registered Nurse, Software Engineer and Accountant each have thousands of EOIs waiting.
Why is my occupation's backlog frozen?
A "Frozen" cutoff means that occupation received no subclass 189 invitations in the latest round — common for ICT and accounting occupations. For these, state nomination (190) or regional (491) pathways are usually the realistic route.
